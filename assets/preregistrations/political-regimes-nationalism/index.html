Political Regimes and the Shape of Nationalism

A conjoint approach to the content of national identity in Mainland China and Taiwan

Status
OSF preregistration, registered November 11, 2025 (updated November 17, 2025). The registration is currently under embargo.
Authors
Steven Denney (Leiden University)
H. Christoph Steinhardt (University of Vienna)
Registration
osf.io/6srfp

Description

A paired conjoint study of how political regimes shape the content of nationalism, comparing authoritarian Mainland China and democratic Taiwan, two societies with sharply diverging regime types but connected histories and a shared cultural tradition. The registration was deposited on OSF before fielding and remains under embargo while data collection and co-author review are completed. Full design and hypothesis details will be posted here once the embargo lifts.
